# Homeware UI skin — 2026-11-06

The admin pages are Lua templates under `/www`. The purple look is `gw.css`. Replacing those templates was not required.

**VERIFIED.** `/etc/nginx/main_genesis.conf` is included by the existing `include main_*.conf` in the port 80/443 server. For `text/html` it clears `Content-Length`, sets `Cache-Control: no-cache`, and inserts `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/css/genesis.css">` before `</head>`. The stylesheet is `/www/docroot/css/genesis.css` on the overlay (`ubi0_20`). `nginx -t` passed and `/etc/init.d/nginx reload` returned 0. `wget` of `http://127.0.0.1/login.lp` contained that link. `/css/genesis.css` was served. A browser load of `http://192.168.1.1/login.lp` showed the dark sign-in card, light buttons, and the Telia mark. Other content types are not modified.

Repo copies, with no device secret in them: `scripts/f1_ui_genesis.css`, `scripts/f1_nginx_main_genesis.conf`.

The first body filter failed to compile (`unexpected symbol near '/'`) and `login.lp` returned an empty reply until the link string was a Lua long string. After the reload, the page loaded.
